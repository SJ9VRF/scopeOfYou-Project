# Scope of You — Novelty Verdict

**Author:** Aura Yavary  
**Date:** 25 September 2026

## Verdict

- **Research question:** compelling and timely.
- **Broad premise (“sometimes do not personalize”):** not novel.
- **Generic contract framing:** not novel.
- **Narrow candidate novelty:** learning the behavioral scope of a *known* preference via jointly enforced APPLY / SUPPRESS / MUST-NOT-AFFECT obligations, matched counterfactual interventions, and permission-sensitive agentic transitions.
- **Current SOTA claim:** not justified; real-LLM/public-benchmark gate is still pending.
- **Portfolio value:** high if presented as falsification-driven research with strict claim boundaries, not as a fabricated frontier-model win.

## Recommended title

**Scope of You: Learning Where Personalization Is Allowed to Matter**

## Hiring-facing pitch

A personalized AI should not treat every remembered preference as a global instruction. Scope of You learns the scope of a known preference effect: when to express it, when context should suppress it, and what protected behavior or action permissions it must not change. The first implementation failed an independently worded implicit evaluation; the project retains that failure, redesigns the training interventions, and measures the resulting selectivity–utility frontier rather than hiding it.
